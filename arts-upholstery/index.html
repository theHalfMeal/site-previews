How a seat gets rebuilt

Five steps. Every stitch by hand.

01 / 05
01 · STRIPDown to the frame

Old covers and tired foam come off, and we check the springs and frame.

02 · PATTERNEvery panel traced

The old cover becomes the template, so the new one fits exactly.

03 · CUTLeather, vinyl or cloth

Matched to factory color, or something new with contrast thread.

04 · STITCHSewn on a walking-foot machine

Double-stitched seams that hold up to years of sliding in and out.

05 · FITBack in, tight and even

New foam, cover stretched and fastened, seat bolted back in.
