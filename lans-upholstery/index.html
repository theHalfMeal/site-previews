How much fabric does it take?

Bringing your own fabric is welcome. These are yardages for plain 54-inch fabric. Add about 15% for stripes or a pattern repeat that has to line up.

How it works

From your living room to like-new.

  1. Send a photo

    Use the form below or call the shop with a picture of the piece and what needs fixing.

  2. Choose your fabric

    Pick from fabric books at the shop on Northwest Blvd, or bring your own.

  3. We do the work

    Frame checked, cushions rebuilt, new fabric fitted and stitched by hand.

  4. Ready for pickup

    We call you when it’s done. Ask about delivery when you book.
